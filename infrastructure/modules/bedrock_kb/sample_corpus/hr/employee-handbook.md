# Employee Handbook

This handbook describes the working norms and conduct expectations of the
organization. It is intended as a living document; team leads update it
quarterly and the People team reviews changes annually.

## Code of Conduct

We expect every team member to act with integrity, respect, and curiosity.

- Treat colleagues, candidates, and customers with consistent professionalism.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people. Strong opinions are welcome; personal
  attacks are not.
- Raise concerns early. Surface blockers in standup, not at the deadline.
- Assume positive intent. If something feels off, ask before escalating.

Violations of the code of conduct are reviewed by the People team. Substantiated
serious violations may result in a documented warning, role change, or
separation.

## Working Hours

Standard working hours are 9:00 to 17:00 in your local time zone, with a one
hour break for lunch. Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 15:00 in your
team's primary time zone.

- Time outside core hours is yours to schedule. Async work is the default; live
  meetings are scheduled deliberately.
- All-hands meetings are held twice per quarter and recorded for any team member
  who cannot attend live.
- On-call rotations are owned by individual engineering teams; rotation
  schedules live in the team's runbook.

## Remote Work Policy

Remote work is the default. Office space is available for any team member who
prefers in-person work or wants a change of environment.

- Pick the working location that lets you do your best work. Coffee shops,
  coworking spaces, and home offices are all acceptable.
- Maintain a reliable internet connection during your stated working hours.
- For roles that require occasional in-person presence (recruiting events,
  customer meetings), travel costs are covered per the expense policy.

## Dress Code

There is no formal dress code. Wear what you would feel comfortable in when
seeing your team. For customer-facing meetings, match the customer's expected
formality.

## Inclusive Language

Choose language that includes everyone in the room. Avoid idioms tied to a
single language or culture when working with global teams. Use gender-neutral
phrasing in written documentation. The Style Guide on the internal wiki
maintains the current list of preferred terms.

## Reporting Concerns

Any team member may raise a concern through their manager, their manager's
manager, the People team, or the anonymous reporting channel. All reports are
reviewed within five business days. Retaliation against a reporter is itself a
violation of this code.
