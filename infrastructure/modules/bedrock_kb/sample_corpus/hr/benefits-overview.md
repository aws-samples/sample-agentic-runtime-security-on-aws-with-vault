# Benefits Overview

This document summarizes the benefits available to full-time team members.
Part-time and contract roles are covered under a separate addendum. All numbers
below are illustrative and intended for the workshop scenario only.

## Health Insurance

Three plan tiers are offered. Choose at hire and change once per year during
open enrollment, or within 30 days of a qualifying life event.

- **Bronze** — Lowest monthly premium. Higher deductible. Best for team members
  who rarely visit a provider and want catastrophic coverage.
- **Silver** — Mid-tier premium and deductible. Includes preventive dental and
  basic vision.
- **Gold** — Highest monthly premium. Lowest deductible and copays. Includes
  comprehensive dental, vision, and a higher mental-health visit allowance.

Domestic partners and dependents may be added to any tier at the published
family rate.

## Retirement Plan

The organization sponsors a defined-contribution retirement plan with employer
matching.

- Employer match formula: 100% of the first 4% contributed, plus 50% of the
  next 2% contributed.
- Maximum employer contribution is therefore 5% of eligible compensation when a
  team member contributes 6% or more.
- Vesting is immediate. Contributions are yours from day one.
- Investment lineup is reviewed annually by the plan committee.

## Parental Leave

Parental leave is available to all full-time team members regardless of role,
gender, or path to parenthood (birth, adoption, or foster placement).

- 12 weeks of paid leave at full salary.
- May be taken in one continuous block or split across the first 12 months
  after the qualifying event.
- Coordinated with manager and People team at least 30 days in advance when
  practical.

## Wellness Stipend

Each full-time team member receives a $50 monthly wellness stipend. Eligible
expenses include gym memberships, fitness apps, mental-health apps, sports
equipment, and ergonomic home-office accessories.

Submit receipts via the standard expense flow with the category set to
"Wellness". Unused balance does not roll over.

## Learning Budget

Each team member has an annual learning budget of $2,500 for books, courses,
conferences, and certifications. Manager approval is required for items over
$500. The budget resets every January and does not roll over.

## Employee Assistance Program

A 24/7 confidential counseling and referral service is available for all team
members and their immediate family. The service is free at point of use; the
organization does not receive individual usage data.
