# Paid Time Off Policy

This policy describes how paid time off is accrued, requested, and approved.
PTO covers vacation, personal time, and short-term illness. Sick leave is
included in the same balance; there is no separate sick-leave bucket.

## PTO Accrual

PTO accrues monthly based on tenure as of the first day of each calendar
month.

- **0 to 2 years of tenure** — 15 days per year (1.25 days accrued per month).
- **2 to 5 years of tenure** — 20 days per year (1.67 days accrued per month).
- **5 or more years of tenure** — 25 days per year (2.08 days accrued per
  month).

Accrual continues during paid leave (parental, jury duty) and pauses during
unpaid leave.

## Carry-Over Cap

Up to 10 days of unused PTO carry over from one calendar year to the next. Any
balance above 10 days as of December 31 is forfeited. The People team sends
reminders in October and November to team members who would forfeit time.

## Blackout Dates

There are no organization-wide blackout dates. Individual teams may set their
own quiet periods (for example, around a major release) and communicate them
in the team handbook. Quiet periods do not override approved leave already on
the calendar.

## Requesting Time Off

Submit PTO requests in the time-tracking system at least one week in advance
for routine time off, and three weeks in advance for any block of five or more
consecutive days. Requests longer than ten consecutive days require manager
plus People-team approval.

Approval is generally automatic when team coverage is in place. Conflicts are
resolved in the order requests were submitted.

## Bereavement Leave

Five days of paid bereavement leave are available per qualifying event for the
loss of an immediate family member. Two additional days are available for
extended family. Bereavement leave does not draw from the PTO balance.

## Sabbatical

Team members who reach five years of continuous tenure are eligible for a
four-week paid sabbatical. The sabbatical must be taken within the year
following the five-year anniversary; it does not bank for later use.

Sabbaticals require 90 days of advance notice and are coordinated with the
manager and People team to ensure team coverage. Sabbaticals are encouraged
to be a true break from work; on-call rotations are reassigned for the
duration.

## Unlimited Sick Time for Serious Illness

Long-term medical leave for a serious health condition is handled separately
from PTO accrual through short-term and long-term disability programs.
Contact the People team for details.
