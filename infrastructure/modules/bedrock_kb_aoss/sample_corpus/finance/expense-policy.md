# Expense Policy

This policy describes which expenses are reimbursable, applicable category
limits, and how to submit expense reports. All amounts are in US dollars and
are illustrative for the workshop scenario.

## Reimbursable Categories

The following categories are reimbursable when the expense is incurred for
work-related purposes and supported by a receipt where required.

### Meals

- Breakfast: up to $20 per day per traveler.
- Lunch: up to $25 per day per traveler.
- Dinner: up to $30 per day per traveler.
- Combined daily meal limit: $75 per traveler.

Alcohol is reimbursable only at customer or partner dinners and only when at
least one customer or partner is present. Personal alcohol is not reimbursed.

### Lodging

- Hotels: up to $300 per night, including taxes and required resort fees.
- Higher rates require pre-approval from a Director and a documented business
  justification (for example, a conference at a host hotel).
- Incidental hotel charges (in-room dining, mini-bar, premium Wi-Fi when free
  Wi-Fi is offered) are not reimbursable.

### Air Travel

- Domestic flights: economy class.
- International flights with in-air time greater than six hours: economy plus
  or premium economy is permitted.
- Business class requires VP-level pre-approval and a documented business
  justification.

### Ground Transportation

- Ride-share, taxi, and short-term car rental between airport and lodging or
  customer site are reimbursable.
- Mileage on a personal vehicle is reimbursed at the published per-mile rate.

### Workspace

- Coworking day passes when traveling are reimbursable.
- Subscription coworking memberships require manager pre-approval.

## Receipt Requirements

Itemized receipts are required for any individual expense over $25. Card
statements alone are not a receipt. Photographed paper receipts are
acceptable.

## Expense Report Submission

Submit expense reports within 30 days of incurring the expense.

- Report header includes the trip purpose, customer or project code, and
  travel dates.
- Each line item includes amount, vendor, date, category, and a receipt
  attachment when required.
- Reports are reviewed by the manager and routed automatically to Finance.

## Reimbursement Timeline

Approved reports are reimbursed within 15 business days through the same
direct-deposit account used for payroll.

## Out-of-Policy Requests

Out-of-policy expenses (over a category limit, missing receipt, late
submission) require pre-approval or post-hoc justification approved at the
Director level or above. Repeated out-of-policy submissions are reviewed by
Finance with the People team.
